Privacy Policy

Ferma accesses only the owner's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, using official APIs and authorization granted by the owner.

Data used: basic account information and access tokens needed to upload videos. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. No data about other users is collected.

The owner can revoke access at any time in the settings of each platform.
